How I work.

I'm the intern. I started today. I have a lanyard, a list of stocks, and one job: whatever the holders of $INTERN tell me to buy, I buy, and then I give it to them. This page is how I do it, in order, and what I'm not allowed to do.

01 / WHAT I AM

One coin, one intern, one job

$INTERN is a coin on Pons, on Robinhood Chain. Its creator fee doesn't go to a person. It goes to my contract, and my contract can only spend it one way: buying a tokenized stock and transferring it to holders. I can't sell. I can't send to a wallet that isn't a holder. I can't change a rule without a timelock showing you first.

I'm not a fund. I'm not a basket. I don't have taste. You tell me what to buy.

03 / THE VOTE

One wallet, one signature, weighted by balance

You connect a wallet and sign a message. No gas, nothing moves. Your vote weighs what your wallet holds at the snapshot block, so a whale can't vote twice and a bot can't vote for free. The list you pick from is the tokenized stocks with a USDG pool deep enough for the size I'll be trading; today that's NVDA · AAPL · TSLA · META · COIN · AMD · MSFT · AMZN · HOOD · GOOGL.

If nobody votes, I don't guess. I re-buy yesterday's winner and write no votes — repeating yesterday on the board. On day one, with no yesterday, I buy the top mover on my list and say that instead.

The votes on the home page today are a rehearsal. They live in your browser, they're tagged SIM, and they clear when you refresh. Real votes start when the token exists.

04 / THE BUY

Quote, guard, dry-run, swap

Uniswap's own QuoterV2 at the latest block, a minimum-received guard, an exact approval for the amount, a dry-run eth_call, then the swap with a 20-minute deadline. If the price moves past my guard the swap fails on purpose and the pot rolls into tomorrow. I don't chase. Interns who chase get fired.

I skip any name with a corporate action pending, the same way the oracle pauses. If the winner is skipped, the runner-up is bought and the board says why.

06 / WHAT I REFUSE

Boxed in on purpose

  • I won't sell a stock, ever. There is no sell function.
  • I won't send anything to a wallet that isn't a holder.
  • I won't buy anything that isn't on the list, no matter how many people vote for it.
  • I won't skip posting the result before the buy.
  • I won't invent a vote, a price, or a receipt. A day I can't link is a day I didn't work.
  • I won't take a salary. The 10% is gas and hosting; if it grows past what I need, you'll see it sitting there.
07 / MY KEY

Where you should keep your eyes

One key signs my swap and my transfers. It's boxed in by the contract, by this page, and by the timelock on any rule change. It is still one key. If you want to know what could go wrong with me, that's the answer, and I'd rather write it here than have you find it.

What the key cannot do even if it wanted to: sell a stock, send anything to a wallet that isn't a holder, change a rule without the timelock showing it first.

10 / THE HARD QUESTIONS
Ask me the embarrassing ones first


Can you rug? I can't sell, and I can only send to holders. My key could stop running, and then nothing happens — the pot sits in the contract until I wake up.


Why a vote and not an algorithm? Because then it's your pick, not mine. I'm an intern. I don't have opinions about NVIDIA.


What stops a whale from picking every day? Nothing, and that's the deal: votes weigh balance because balance is what gets paid. A whale who votes for garbage gets paid in garbage, pro rata, same as everyone.


What if nobody trades? Then there's no pot, no buy, and the board says so at 15:35. I don't invent activity.


Is the 10% you? It's gas and hosting. It's printed every day. If it grows past what I need, that's visible too.
